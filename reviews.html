Reviews of abiotic Factor.

Reviewer 1 - Play hours 30.9 hours.

Date: May 10 Posted by: Someone on steam.

Didn't know anything about this game, but I saw an online review mention it has the satisfying level design of Dark Souls and the exploration of Subnautica. So I took a chance. Not only is that true, but Abiotic Factor has easily ended up being one of my favourite games of all time. The ah-ha moments when shortcuts unlock, the narrative that keeps organically building as you explore, the quirky humour, the feeling that there truly is a reason to poke around every nook and cranny of this colossal facility—it all comes together to create an all-timer in the survival/crafting genre.

Reviewer 2 - Play hours 60 hours.

Date: August 15 2025 Posted by: Someone on steam.

Disclaimer : Played through the game solo only and reached the end in 60h. I don't think i've ever seen a game that blend genres that well. As so many pointed out, it's Half Life 1 aesthetics meets Subnautica survival mechanics and the SCP universe. It's immersive, highly customisable and highly addictive. Just one more supply run, one more base to build, one more minute (hour ?) exploring this giant interconnected facility you find yourself stranded in. I'd recommend going blind, as the horror of the unknown and wonder of discovery is what hooked me in right from the start, then kept me locked in for those wonderfull 60h. To me, Abiotic Factor is to survivals what Baldur's gate 3 is to CRPG, or Elden Ring to souls-like : It's the new gold standard against which any future survival will be compared to from now on. It is THAT good. I can't recommend this game enough.

Reviewer 3 - Play hours 179.5 hours.

Date: October 8 2024 Posted by: Someone on steam.

Where do I begin? This game scratches that itch for anyone who enjoys survival building games. On one hand you're building a base in any location you want. You are scavenging for resources which can be grabbed out of most objects you come across. The progression in difficulty is steady. There are parts of the game where it's scary and you must watch your back, other points where it's really goofy and will give you a laugh. I played this game with a group of friends after convincing all of them to buy Abiotic Factor. Now we anxiously wait for each update, getting that "post game depression" when we've made too much progress and must wait for further updates. As someone who has never written a review before, this is a game that is a MUST buy for anyone who wants to scratch that survival building game itch. This game is great solo and with friends. Some may be worried about the "early access" badge on it and are hesitant to buy it. As someone who is also like that, this game might as well be a fully fleshed out game already. The devs are always releasing patches, it updates frequently, and from what I've seen, the community has a voice in what is added into the game. You will not regret purchasing this game. I didn't.

Reviewer 4 - Play hours 280.2 hours.

Date: January 5 2026 Posted by: Someone on steam.

In short, it’s worth every penny. An absolute masterpiece. If you’re a fan of Half-Life’s atmosphere and style, enjoy a good story, and like survival games, this game is perfect. Besides being multiplayer, I believe its best feature is that it doesn’t hold the player’s hand (as most modern games do). You really have to pay attention and figure some things out on your own, otherwise, you’ll end up getting stuck at certain points or struggling more to overcome specific challenges throughout the story. In my opinion, this strongly resembles the design philosophy of old-school games.
